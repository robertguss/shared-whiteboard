## What was done

1. The agent extracted Robert's own typed messages from the agent session records on this machine: about 104,000 tokens across 23 workspaces, 29 October to 15 November 2026.
2. Four reader agents each read one part and listed ideas restated as mechanisms: the Mo sessions, one-off brainstorm sessions, the other project workspaces, and the GitHub account (about 415 of Robert's own repositories).
3. A council of three models from three providers (Claude Opus 5.5, GPT 6.1 Sol, Gemini 4 Carbon) each read the four findings independently and answered the same five questions.

## What is and is not in this repo

The findings and the council's answers are private and are kept outside this repo. They contain Robert's private projects and personal matters. This repo holds only what the test taught about the design, in `wiki/hand-run-test.md`.

## Limits

- The sessions cover 17 days. More than a year of sessions is on another machine and was not read.
- About half of the "user" messages in the session records were written by an orchestrating agent, not typed by Robert.
- Most repositories were judged from their names and descriptions.
- Robert has not yet judged whether the connections found are real, new or useful.
