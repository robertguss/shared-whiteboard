Date: 15 November 2026. Follows `2026-11-15-north-star-open.md`.

## What the agent asked

For a real moment of returning to the Mo project after time away, to find the north star in an actual frustration.

## What Robert said, in their words

Dictated. Filler words are removed and nothing else is changed. "Zetal cost in" in the transcription is read as Zettelkasten.

> Since this project has kind of shifted quite a bit from being like a visual whiteboard or a surface to better understand things between the agent and the human and getting more down into the database and storage layer. You kind of have to think about it a little bit differently and I don't want to get too myopic and focused on just like one particular project like Mo. This is really more like, for example, all the projects that I work on. And all of the ideas that I have. A lot of them overlap. And relate and inform one another. But I don't really have a centralized place where all of those connections can be made except for my own human brain. But even then it's difficult for me to always see the connections. So what I'm essentially looking for is like, a database that almost acts like an external form of my brain, but that the AI agent can read and interact with and understand and make connections or propose them. Et cetera, et cetera. Not quite, I don't want to get into the language of the whole 2nd brain thing because that gets into the whole PKM movement, and like obsidian and stuff, which there's certainly overlap. With those ideas. But I am thinking of something much bigger and deeper and more ambitious than just connecting notes and marking notes in markdown or a Zettelkasten or something like that.

## Decided

- The scope is all of Robert's projects and ideas, not one project. Mo is one part of it, not the frame.
- The thing is a database that acts as an external form of Robert's brain, which an AI agent can read, interact with and understand, and in which the agent makes or proposes connections.
- The pain it answers: ideas across projects overlap and inform one another, there is no one place where those connections can be made, and Robert cannot always see them unaided.
- It is not to be framed as a "second brain" or as personal knowledge management. Robert sees overlap with those and wants something bigger, deeper and more ambitious than linked notes.

## Not decided

- The north star in one sentence. This statement is the closest so far, and the agent proposes wording from it.
- What "bigger and deeper" consists of. Robert has said what it is not.

## What the agent takes from it

- The earlier question "one brain or one per project" is answered: one.
- A person's whole body of ideas is private. The design and the engine can stay in this public repo; the contents of the brain cannot. Proposed, not confirmed.
- The visual views chosen earlier (shape B) remain ways of showing the data. They are no longer the centre of the project.
