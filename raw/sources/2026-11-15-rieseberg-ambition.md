## Summary

An essay on asking more of AI models. It names four kinds of ambition and gives techniques for three.

| Kind | Meaning | Techniques given |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Speed | The same work in less time | Skipped by the author as well established |
| Altitude | Aim at the outcome, not the task | Ask what should happen next and what the goal beyond the goal is |
| Depth | Do the task the way a field-leading expert would | Use the experts' instruments, set unreasonable goals, challenge assumptions against raw data, produce new knowledge, use an outside judge |
| Range | Draw on more fields than the task shows on its surface | Replace nouns with verbs, assemble a crew of fields, borrow their methods and not their voices |

The closing section argues that people are held back by what they believe possible, and cites goal-setting research that specific hard goals beat "do your best".
